Underlying data
---------------
Images are cutouts from the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS), g/r/i bands.
Morphology labels originate from Galaxy Zoo, crowdsourced from volunteer
classifications.

Changes made in this dataset
----------------------------
As required by CC-BY-4.0, the modifications are stated explicitly:

1. Two of the ten original Galaxy10 classes were REMOVED for rarity --
   class 3 "Smooth, Cigar shaped" (349 images) and class 5 "Disk, Edge-on,
   Boxy Bulge" (17 images). Neither has enough examples to train or to target.
   The remaining 8 classes were re-indexed 0-7; classes.csv records the mapping
   back to the original Galaxy10 indices.
2. Remaining classes were capped at 2500 images each to limit a 13:1 class
   imbalance down to roughly 4.7:1.
3. Images were re-encoded from the source HDF5 array to PNG, unchanged in
   pixel content, size (69x69) or band ordering.
4. Images were split into two disjoint pools. One pool trains a classifier that
   is NOT released; only the other pool is shipped here.

Derived content
---------------
responses.npy, ops.csv, catalog.py, meta.csv and meta.json are original work
created for this challenge. They record the measured behaviour of a
purpose-trained classifier under a calibrated catalog of observing conditions,
and are not part of, nor derived from, the Galaxy10 labels. They are released
under the same CC-BY-4.0 terms for convenience.
